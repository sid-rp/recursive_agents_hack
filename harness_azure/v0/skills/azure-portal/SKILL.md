---
name: azure-portal
description: How the Azure portal is laid out and the fastest ways to open services and blades
---
- The global search bar at the top ("Search resources, services, and docs") opens any service by name: type the
  service name, then click the matching item under "Services".
- "Home" (portal.azure.com/#home) shows Azure services and recent resources; the menu (three lines, top left) lists
  services too.
- Pages open as "blades" to the right; the breadcrumb at the top goes back; the X at the top right closes a blade.
- List pages have a toolbar (+ Create, Refresh, ...) and a filter box above the grid.
- Slow blades show loading dots: wait, then observe again.
